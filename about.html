RESUME

I’m Yeojin, a product designer, design engineer, and strategist working across product, technology, and digital experience. I’ve helped teams at Google, Air Canada, and YouTube bring products from ideas to experiences people actually use.

Currently at Air Canada, I lead mobile experiences across iOS and Android, shaping products from strategy and discovery through design and delivery. I work closely with product and engineering to simplify complex problems and turn ideas into meaningful digital experiences.

My work brings together design, user research, data, technology, and AI to make informed decisions and create experiences that are clear, intuitive, and natural. I enjoy getting close to the technology, using code and emerging tools to prototype, experiment, and bring ideas to life.

I’m drawn to the details that shape how a product feels, creating experiences that are thoughtful, calm, and human.
